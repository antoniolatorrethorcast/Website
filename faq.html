What is Thorcast.AI?

Thorcast.AI is a predictive analytics platform with AI agents. NORA prepares your data and MJÖ builds, compares and explains the models, from raw files to a model in production. It is built for banks, insurers, insurance brokers, telcos and retailers.

Who is Thorcast.AI for?

Analytics and risk teams of any size. Small teams use it to do the work of a larger data science team; larger teams use it to cut the time from data to decision and to explore more use cases with the same people.

What kind of models can I build?

Credit risk for origination and collections, fraud detection, churn and retention, cross-sell and next best offer, lead qualification, customer lifetime value, pricing and loss cost in insurance, demand forecasting, income estimation and geographic models. If your data can support a prediction, Thorcast can model it.

Do I need to write code?

No. You tell NORA in plain language what the data needs (clean, join, build the target) and approve its plan; MJÖ then plans the preprocessing, recommends variables, trains and compares several algorithms and presents the best model. You still get reproducible Python and a reusable Playbook for every data preparation.

What are NORA and MJÖ?

They are Thorcast's AI agents. NORA is the data preparation agent: it merges, cleans and engineers your tables, and every step is checked by a deterministic validation engine. MJÖ is the AI data scientist: it advises step by step or runs a mission you approve once, compares algorithms with AutoML, explains the results and applies decision layers.

Where does Thorcast run, and does my data leave my company?

Models can run in Thorcast Cloud, on your own premises, or right in the app. Data preparation, model training and scoring can run in your environment, on your infrastructure, so sensitive data does not leave your premises.

What is the Data Enrichment Network?

It adds data your model does not have today: more than 3,000 data points condensed into 261 model-ready signals, starting from keys you already hold, such as an address, an email or a phone number. You choose which families to add: Core Signals, Deep Signals, Geo Intelligence and, on request, Credit Bureau data.

How much does alternative data improve a model?

Across 68 models in 12 countries, adding alternative data raised the institution's own Gini by a median of 35%, and all 68 models improved. The less credit history customers have, the larger the gain: a median of +11% for customers with a bureau hit, +32% for thin-file customers and +36% for customers with no bureau history. On their own, without bureau data, Core + Deep Signals reach a Gini of 0.31 for customers with little or no history.

Does Thorcast work outside Latin America?

Yes. The platform works with your own data in any region: the Americas, Europe, Africa and Asia. The enrichment results above include models in Spain, the Czech Republic, South Africa and the United Arab Emirates. Data enrichment coverage depends on the country, so ask us about yours.

How are models explained and audited?

Every model comes with industry-standard metrics (Gini, KS, ROC), a plain-language explanation from MJÖ and an audit-ready technical report. The AI plans and validated code executes, so the same input gives the same result every time, and every step is logged and reproducible.

How do scores turn into decisions?

Decision layers convert a score into a policy: for each cutoff you see approval, risk and economic impact side by side. You can also run batch scoring and compare a new model against the current one with Champion–Challenger.

What happens after the model is in production?

Thorcast monitors real-world performance, detects drift and helps you recalibrate when the population changes, using Champion–Challenger to measure the improvement.

How does it connect to my systems?

You connect your internal data in minutes, with no migration, and load files such as CSV, Parquet or Excel. Results are available through APIs, dashboards, or directly in Slack and Microsoft Teams.

How fast is it?

From your data to a world-class decision in under 15 minutes for a standard project. A proof of concept that measures how much enriched data improves your current score takes about an hour.

How can I try Thorcast?

Start with a proof of concept: prepare a sample with an ID, email, phone, address, your current score and the real outcome. Thorcast enriches it, trains a challenger model and compares it with your score. Write to info@thorcast.ai, or watch the tutorials first.
